What the numbers are

Every player gets a distribution, not a point. Proj is the median: the score that half of outcomes beat and half fall short of. Floor and Ceiling are the 10th and 90th percentiles. The Mean is the expected value, which is what you add up for a lineup and what ESPN's number is comparable to. The model is validated each week against what happened and against a season-average baseline; the week's results are in the repository's FINDINGS.md.

Where a projection comes from

  • Usage: trailing three games of touches, targets, shares and red-zone work, weighted by snap share; lead-back or lead-receiver status; a teammate who is out this week.
  • The player: draft pick, experience and prior-season efficiency. This is what separates a back thrust into volume from one who has earned it.
  • The game: Vegas spread and total, pace, pass rate.
  • The opponent: each defense's measured effect on quarterbacks, applied equally to the quarterback and every pass catcher facing it (Opp adj), plus wind. Explosive-run susceptibility moves running-back ceilings only.

Tiers and usage labels

Tiers are rank buckets of the mean (RB1 = top 12, RB2 = 13–24) with sub-tiers (a, b, c) wherever there is a gap of 1.5+ points. The Usage label is independent of the projection: elite / high / mid / low from snap share and carry or target share. Where usage is ahead of the projection the model is skeptical of the output; where the projection is ahead of usage, the model is betting on the player's pedigree or a role change.

Flags

  • emerging: trailing average at least 4 points above the season average.
  • soft run D: opponent is in the bottom third at preventing explosive runs; lifts the back's ceiling.
  • shootout: both offenses top-third, spread within 3.5, no top-7 pass defense.
  • tough pass D: opponent's effect on quarterbacks is in the toughest 15%.
  • wind: 12+ mph forecast; extreme: heavy rain or 25+ mph, the passing game may not exist.
  • new QB: a quarterback change this week, which costs pass catchers about 2.5 points on average regardless of who the new starter is.
